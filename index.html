About me

Started in design. Now I lead omnichannel.

M.Sc ChemistryIISc BengaluruExecutive MBA, IIM NagpurEMERGE, Denmark

I plan the campaigns, design the screens and make the films that reach 30,000+ doctors, on the channels they already use.

Today I lead omnichannel engagement (OCE) at Novo Nordisk India for Rybelsus, Wegovy, Ozempic and Awiqli. I take a brand plan off the slide and into the field: the content, the calendar and the campaigns across broadcast emails, rep-triggered emails, WhatsApp, the HCP portal and Digital Sales, on Veeva and with partners like Doceree and Docplexus. I have also managed the National Sales Meet and product launches.

I started in design in February 2023. I led the UI/UX of the India HCP Portal, designed conversations for the NIAA WhatsApp chatbot and built the e-learning experience behind WE Learn Obesity. That design start still powers my work: an AI workflow I set up lifted our asset output 3.5x, to more than 1,000 a year.
