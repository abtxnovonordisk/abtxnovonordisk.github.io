About
Me

Designer, motion maker and AI tinkerer, with an M.Sc in Chemistry that helps me speak science with medical teams. Now studying marketing at IIM Nagpur.

Since February 2023 I have shaped how Novo Nordisk India talks to doctors online. I led the UI/UX of the India HCP Portal, designed conversations for the NIAA WhatsApp chatbot, and built the e-learning experience behind WE Learn Obesity.

Today I run omnichannel engagement (OCE). I take a brand plan off the slide and into the field: the content, the calendar and the campaigns across BCE emails, RTEs and WhatsApp, all built to back the sales team. An AI workflow I set up lifted our asset output 3.5x, to more than 1,000 a year.

I plan the campaigns, design the screens and make the films that reach 30,000+ doctors, on the channels they already use.
